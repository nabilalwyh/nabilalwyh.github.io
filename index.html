Competitions

Competition

In the midst of my busy college schedule, I am also actively participating in several competitions, both individually and as part of a team.

2nd place winner - The Pitching Day by Competemate - Tech and Innovation

Competemate is the competition organized by Google Developer Group on Campus Gunadarma University in 2025. In this competition, my role is Hustler leading business strategy, pitch deck development, team coordination, and delivered the final pitch presentation to judges.

3rd place winner - Unlimited Software Building - ICT Business

Unlimited Software Building is the competition organized by the Information System Laboratory (LabSI) of Gunadarma University in 2024. In this competition, my role is CTO of cetak.io defining the technical vision and strategy for the application, ensuring it aligns with the overall business objectives.I also developed the UI/UX prototype for the cetak.io application

3rd place winner - KOMPRES 15 - IoT

KOMPRES 15 is the competition organized by the Informatics Laboratory (LabIF) of Gunadarma University in 2024. In this competition, my role is leader of group Pengagum Rahasia responsible for leading project named Smart Library System. We provide a device that can read the identification cards of borrowers and the books they want to borrow, together with a website that allows librarians to keep track of book loan information.

Top 33 - UI Digitalk 2024 - UI/UX Competition

UI Digitalk 2024 is the competition organized by the Student Executive Board (BEM) of Universitas Indonesia in 2024. In this competition, my role is a leader of group Pengagum Rahasia responsible on leading a project named WellNes. WellNess is a fitness application that combines IoT and AI technology to help users who face challenges in leading a healthy lifestyle, such as a lack of motivation to exercise and irregular sleep patterns.

3rd place winner - PT Maxima Performa Indonesia - Popular Article Writing Competition.

In this competition, I wrote an article about the work environment.
